# Puff Note Design Direction

Overall feeling:
Japanese editorial minimalism meets soft cloud journaling.

The page should feel:
quiet, poetic, premium, soft, tactile, airy, slightly playful, refined.

The page should not feel:
SaaS, ecommerce, air purifier advertisement, generic wellness app, childish kawaii, busy scrapbook, overdesigned.

Visual references:
1. Japanese editorial calm
2. Soft cloud atmosphere
3. 217 Atelier taste
4. Tactile mochi bubble buttons

Palette:
Background: #F8F6F1
Primary text: #1C1C1C
Secondary text: #6F6F6F
Quiet labels: #A7A7A7
Mist blue: #EAF1F7
Pale lilac: #EEE9F6
Blush: #F7E9EC
Cream: #FAF6EF
Soft grey: #F2F3F5
Soft green: #E8EFE4
Warm peach: #F7E8DD

Typography:
Headings: Playfair Display or Cormorant Garamond
Body and UI: Inter

Hero copy:
A soft space
for your mind
to breathe.

Puff Note is a mood journaling companion.
Capture feelings as they are, and let them drift,
shift, and settle.

Mood prompt:
How are you, right now?

Mood labels:
Calm
Dreamy
Reflective
Soft
Curious

Lower section:
MADE TO FEEL, NOT FORCED

Puff Note helps you notice what matters,
without pressure or performance.
Just honest moments, beautifully held.

Quote:
Some days are just for feeling.
That’s okay.

Bubble button direction:
Mood buttons should feel like soft mochi, satin glass, or jelly pebbles.
They should feel pressable, but not childish.
They should have translucent pastel fill, internal glow, soft shadow, and gentle sink-in pressed state.
